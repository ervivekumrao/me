Privacy Commitment.

Last Updated: March 05, 2025

At Med Reminder, your health data is your most private asset. We are committed to protecting it with the highest standards of security and transparency.

Med Reminder is built with a local-first approach. Most of your data stays on your device, and we never sell your personal or health information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional and reliable reminder service, we manage the following types of data:

Local Health Data

Medication names, dosages, and schedules are stored locally on your device using a secure database. This data is used to trigger reminders and track your adherence history.

User Profiles

If you create profiles for family members, the names and icons associated with these profiles are stored locally to personalize the app experience.

Notification Data

The app uses the Android Alarm Manager and Notification service to provide timely alerts. These services process time and medication labels to show you the right information at the right time.

2. Permissions and Their Use

Med Reminder requires certain permissions to function effectively:

  • Exact Alarm Permission: Needed to trigger medication reminders at the precise time specified.
  • Notification Permission: Essential for displaying reminders and quick actions (Taken, Skip, Snooze) on your lock screen.
  • Post-Reboot Execution: Allows the app to reschedule your alarms automatically after your device restarts.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Since the majority of your data is stored locally, its security also depends on your device's overall security (e.g., screen locks, encryption).

4. Third-Party Services

We may use minimal third-party tools for crash reporting and app performance monitoring to ensure the app remains reliable. These tools do not collect your personal health information.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete any medication or profile at any time within the app. Clearing the app's data in your device settings will permanently remove all locally stored health information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@medreminder.com.
