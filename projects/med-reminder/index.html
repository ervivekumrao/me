Patient Setup Guide

Take control of your health journey in four simple steps.

1

Create Your Profile

Open the app and set up your primary profile. You can also add family members with custom icons for easy identification.

2

Add Medications

Tap "Add Medicine". Use the camera to scan the label or manually enter the details using our intelligent auto-complete.

3

Configure Reminders

Set precise alarm times. Enable "Post-Reboot Execution" to ensure your schedule is never interrupted by device restarts.

4

Track & Adjust

Log your doses as you take them. View the Adherence Log to see your progress and identify patterns over time.
