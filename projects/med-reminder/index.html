Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to begin your health journey.

1

Create Your Profile

Open the app and set up your primary user profile with a name and an icon of your choice.

2

Add Medications

Tap the + button to add a medication. Enter the name, dosage, and set your reminder times.

3

Enable Notifications

Ensure notification permissions are granted so you can receive timely reminders even when the app is closed.

4

Stay Consistent

Log your doses as you take them to build a clear history of your medication adherence.
