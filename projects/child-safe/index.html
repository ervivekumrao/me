Parental Setup Guide

Configure your child's device for maximum safety in minutes.

1

Device Pairing via QR

Install the app on the child's device and pair it with your parental account using the QR code found in your web dashboard.

2

Configure Weekly Limits

Set personalized screen time quotas for each day of the week, with custom allowances for educational and entertainment apps.

3

Activate Kiosk Mode

Select allowed apps and lock the device into a safe study environment backed by the persistent background service.

4

Monitor & Safeguard

Receive real-time location snapshots, app activity logs, and instant alerts across all registered family devices.
