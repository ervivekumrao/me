Safety First

Comprehensive tools to protect and guide your child's digital journey.

🛡️

High-Security Kiosk

Restrict the device to a single app or a pre-approved "Safe List." Prevent accidental exits and unauthorized setting changes using Device Owner mode.

🛰️

Live Radar Tracking

Monitor your child's device location in real-time with geofencing alerts. Get notified instantly when they arrive at school or home.

⏳

Set daily limits and recurring schedules. Automatically lock the device during bedtime or study hours to promote healthy digital habits.

📱

App & Web Filtering

Remotely block inappropriate applications or websites. Set timers for specific entertainment categories to maintain focus on educational tasks.

Parental Setup Guide

Configure your child's device for maximum safety in minutes.

1

Device Pairing

Install the app on the child's device and pair it with your parental account using the QR code found in your web dashboard.

2

Activate Kiosk Mode

Select the allowed applications for the child's device. Toggle "Kiosk Mode" to lock the device into the safe interface.

3

Set Safety Zones

Define Geofence boundaries on the map. Configure push notifications so you're alerted whenever the child enters or leaves these zones.

4

Monitor & Adjust

Log in to the web dashboard at any time to view real-time location, usage reports, and update restrictions remotely.
