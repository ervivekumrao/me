Mockup Master: Dev Tool

The ultimate utility for Android developers and QA engineers. Instantly populate any device with realistic, high-quality mock data for edge-case testing and demo preparation.

Play Store Official Site

Powerful Injections

👤 Rich Contacts

Generate thousands of contacts with avatars, multi-line numbers, and diverse metadata formats.

💬 SMS Simulation

Create deep message threads with varying states: Sent, Received, Draft, and MMS attachments.

📞 Call Log Population

Inject realistic call history including incoming, missed, and blocked events with randomized durations.

🧹 Smart Cleanup

Track and remove only the mock data created by the tool, leaving your personal data untouched.

Workflow

01. Authorization

Grant temporary system permissions for Contacts and SMS. Use the "Default App" toggle for deep SMS injection.

02. Configuration

Select your dataset size and parameters. Use pre-defined "Stress Test" profiles for high-volume scenarios.

03. Execution

Watch the real-time population of your device. Once finished, export your custom datasets for team-wide use.
