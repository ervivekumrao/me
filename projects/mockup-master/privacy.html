Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Welcome to Mockup Master: Dev Tool. This application is a specialized utility designed for Android developers and QA engineers to generate high-fidelity mock data. Due to its nature as a system-level testing tool, it requires access to sensitive device databases.

1. Sensitive Permissions Disclosure

To enable the generation of mock data in system databases, the app requires the following permissions:

Comprehensive Disclosure: The app accesses and modifies Contacts (WRITE_CONTACTS), SMS (WRITE_SMS), and Call Logs (WRITE_CALL_LOG) to facilitate development and testing.

How we use these permissions:

  • Mock Contact Generation: Allows developers to create bulk contact entries to test contact-based features in their own applications.
  • Mock SMS/MMS: Allows for the creation of mock message threads to test notification and messaging logic.
  • Mock Call History: Generates call log entries to test dialer and history-related features.

Strict Scope: Mockup Master only interacts with data that it has specifically created. It maintains a local registry of generated entries to ensure they can be safely cleared without affecting your actual personal data.

2. Data Privacy & Local Processing

We believe your personal data should never leave your device.

  • No External Transmission: Mockup Master does not have any server-side components. No personal data, device identifiers, or generated content is ever transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No Data Collection: We do not collect analytics or usage data from the application.

3. User Control & Safety

As a developer tool, we provide features to ensure your device data remains manageable:

  • Clear All Functionality: The app provides a one-tap solution to remove all mock data it has generated, restoring your system databases to their previous state.
  • Transparency: All operations performed by the app are logged locally for the developer to review.
