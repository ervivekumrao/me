Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Mockup Master: Dev Tool ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

1. Local Processing Only

The App is a developer utility designed for local data generation on your device or emulator. All data generated or processed by the App stays on your device.

2. Permissions Usage

The App requires permissions to write mock data to your device's system databases:

  • Contacts: To create and delete mock contact entries.
  • SMS: To create mock message threads.
  • Call Logs: To generate mock call history.

The App only modifies entries it creates. It tracks these entries in a local database to facilitate the "Clear All" functionality.

3. No External Transmission

The App does not have any server-side components. No personal data, device identifiers, or generated content is ever transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Contact Us

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact manuscriptcode@gmail.com.
