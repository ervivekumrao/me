Workflow Redefined

Engineered for speed, consistency, and automated recruiter record-keeping.

🚀

Smart Calling Automation

Automated dialing queue with configurable 10-second preparation countdowns between calls. Dual mode control lets recruiters pause or resume either from mobile or remotely via the desktop CRM.

🔄

Instant CRM Synchronization

Synchronize your candidate call list with a single tap. All call outcomes, timestamps, incoming/missed history, and notes automatically flow back to the CRM database.

📊

Live Metrics & AHT Tracking

Monitor real-time stats including total Dialed, Connected, and Not Connected calls. Automatically calculates Average Handling Time (AHT) to maintain recruitment quality benchmarks.

📁

Resumes & Audio Recordings

Upload candidate resumes (PDF, Word, Images) directly from your phone to the CRM. Seamlessly attach call audio recordings to candidate profiles for interview audits.

💬

WhatsApp & Quick Feedback

Initiate WhatsApp conversations instantly without saving phone numbers to your device. Post-call presets (Interested, Busy, Call Back) allow rapid note logging.

🔒

Secure 6-Digit Device Pairing

Pair your Android phone securely with the Career Crox CRM using a 6-digit code. Features session validation, remote logout, and enterprise-grade authentication.

Quick Setup Guide

Connect your mobile device to your recruitment CRM in seconds.

1

Secure Device Pairing

Open the app and enter the 6-digit pairing code generated by your Career Crox CRM web dashboard.

2

Sync Calling Queue

Once paired, tap the "Sync" button to pull your assigned candidate list and priority tasks from the CRM.

3

Start Automation

Hit "Start Calling". The app will navigate through your queue, initiating calls with smart cooldowns in between.

4

Document & Close

Upload resumes, link call recordings, and update candidate statuses directly from the post-call interface.
