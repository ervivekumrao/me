Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Career Crox – Calling Assistant ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information Collection

The App requires certain permissions to function as a recruitment tool:

  • Call Logs & Phone: We read call logs to synchronize call outcomes (duration, timestamp, status) with your linked Career Crox CRM. This information is used solely for recruitment tracking.
  • Contacts: Used to identify candidate names from your synced CRM queue.
  • Files & Media: Used if you choose to upload candidate resumes or call recordings to the CRM.

2. Data Usage

Data collected is transmitted only to your securely paired Career Crox CRM instance. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission and a secure 6-digit pairing mechanism to ensure only authorized devices can access your CRM data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@careercrox.com.
