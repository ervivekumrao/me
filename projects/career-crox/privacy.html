Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Welcome to Career Crox – Calling Assistant. This application is designed to help recruitment professionals manage and track candidate interactions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data we access.

1. Sensitive Data Access Disclosure

To function as an effective recruitment calling assistant, the app requires access to several sensitive device features:

Comprehensive Disclosure: The app accesses Call Logs (READ_CALL_LOG) and Phone State (READ_PHONE_STATE) to automate recruitment tracking.

How we use your data:

  • Call Outcome Sync: The app reads call logs to identify recruitment-related calls and synchronizes call details (timestamp, duration, and status) with your linked Career Crox CRM.
  • Caller Identification: Access to phone state allows the app to identify incoming/outgoing calls from candidates in your recruitment queue to provide real-time assistant features.
  • Contact Matching: If granted, contact access is used solely to match CRM candidate profiles with your local contact list for better identification.

2. Data Transmission & Security

We prioritize the security of your professional data during transmission.

  • Secure CRM Pairing: Data is only transmitted to the Career Crox CRM instance that you have securely paired with your device using a unique 6-digit pairing code.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All data sent to your CRM is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or share your call logs or candidate data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. User Control

You maintain full control over the app's data access:

  • Permissions can be revoked at any time through Android System Settings.
  • The app only tracks calls related to your recruitment activities as defined in your CRM configuration.
