Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Office Time Counter ("the App") is committed to protecting employee data privacy.

1. Notification Access

The App requires "Notification Access" to identify check-in and check-out events sent by your organization's companion applications. We only read notifications relevant to time tracking.

2. Data Usage

The time logs generated are stored on your device. If your organization requires syncing, data is sent only to the authorized organizational servers. We do not use this data for any other purposes.

3. No Personal Tracking

The App does not track your location, personal messages, or other device activities unrelated to the specified office time events.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact manuscriptcode@gmail.com.
