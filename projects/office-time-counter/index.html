Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to automate your office logs in minutes.

1

Configure Office Location

Open settings and pin your office location on the map. Set a comfortable radius (e.g., 200m) to trigger the Geofence reliably.

2

Grant Permissions

Enable "Location (Always Allow)" and "Notification Access". These are essential for the app to detect your movements and system events in the background.

3

Set Weekly Targets

Define your required work hours (e.g., 40 hours/week). The app will automatically calculate your progress in real-time.

4

Manual Adjustments

Forget your phone? No problem. Easily add Half-Day, Full-Day, or manual time entries to keep your history accurate.
