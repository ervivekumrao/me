Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Study Companion ("the App") is dedicated to providing a safe and private learning environment.

1. Data Privacy

We prioritize your privacy by keeping your data local. All student profiles, scanned documents, and generated study materials are stored directly on your device.

2. Usage of AI Services

When you use AI features (like Question Generation), the text extracted from your scans may be processed by AI models (such as Gemini or Google ML Kit). This processing is done to provide the requested learning materials and is not used to build profiles about you.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: To scan your textbooks and notes.
  • Storage: To save your study sets and flashcards locally.

4. No Account Required

You can use all features of the App without creating an account or providing personal identification information.

5. Contact Us

For any questions, contact us at manuscriptcode@gmail.com.
